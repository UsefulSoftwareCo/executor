OAuth providers can declare an identity issuer. The host verifies its discovery and authenticated UserInfo subject, groups compatible account bindings within their saved placement, and exposes safe identity metadata without changing account IDs or app selections.

A connection can reuse one covering OAuth grant with the same client and resource. Shared bindings use one encrypted grant and renewal lock; reconnecting or removing one binding preserves the others. Storage 4.0.24 adds nullable references and identity records. Host protocol 23 retains adapters for existing bundles.
